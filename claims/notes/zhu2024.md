# zhu2024 — Zhu et al. 2024, Sci Transl Med

- DOI 10.1126/scitranslmed.abo1997; PMID 39475571; PMCID PMC12372474 (NIH author manuscript, NIHMS2101876)
- Source used: PMC author manuscript via NCBI efetch XML (abstract, all Results subsections, Discussion, Methods, Fig. 1-6 legends). Supplementary tables (S1-S5) and Supplemental Material PDF (figs. S1-S10) not opened; claims citing fig. S panels rely on main-text descriptions.

## Cohort as reported
- Region: prefrontal cortex BA9 (dorsolateral PFC); NKI / NYU Grossman brain bank. Validation RNAscope cohorts from Banner Sun Health and Yale ADRC.
- 6 late-stage PD, 6 sex- and age-matched controls; mean age 75.9 (range 63-96).
- 77,384 nuclei (10x 3' v3); median 2,598 genes, 5,639 UMIs per nucleus.
- 8 major cell types (ExN1-3, InN1-3, Astro, MG, Oligo, OPC, Endo, T cells). T cells: 33 control vs 243 PD nuclei. Microglia: 1,699 vs 3,684.
- Paired label-free proteomics on same tissue (bulk; 2,534 gene symbols; 22 proteins DE at raw P<0.05, none after FDR) — not extracted as claims (bulk).

## Donor metadata available
- Table S1: sex, age, PMI (grouped 5/10/15 h), QC metrics, Lewy body + neurite score (pS129 alpha-synuclein IHC in the same PFC tissue; PD range 0.1-11.9, controls 0).
- Braak stage, disease duration, Hoehn & Yahr, dementia status: not reported in main text (may be in table S1).

## Ambiguities / caveats
- DE: Seurat FindMarkers Wilcoxon on cells (|logFC|>0.25, FDR<0.05); T cells only |logFC|>0.75 with no p-value filter.
- Composition test: Wilcoxon rank-sum on "number (or proportion)" — likely per donor; microglia increase P=0.09 reported as a finding; oligodendrocyte decrease has no statistic.
- Pathology correlation: text says Pearson, Fig. 2 legend says Spearman; n of donors (6 PD or all 12) unclear.
- ExN chaperone list includes HSPH1/HSPB1 as downregulated, but later text says these were exceptions (not down).
- APOE in microglia: Results list it among shared AD/PD upregulated genes; Discussion says downregulated in both.
- Typos in text: "GIGY2" (GIGYF2), "M1M" (MT1M?), "RASGRAF2" (RASGRF2), "PRNK" (PRKN), "HSPA1" (HSPA1A/B).
- Proteomic claims (synaptic modules M1/M2 up, GSTT1/CDH2 up, SEC13/NDRG2 down) are bulk and excluded per schema.
- RNA-velocity claims are qualitative and need unspliced counts (marked not_testable).
