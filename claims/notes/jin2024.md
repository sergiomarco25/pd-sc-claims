## Cohort as reported (post-mortem part)
- Region: superior temporal cortex, grey matter (task brief said "cortex"; recorded as `temporal`). Mayo Clinic Jacksonville brain bank.
- snRNA-seq: 3 controls, 5 LBD (3 with normal SNCA copy number, 1 SNCA duplication, 1 SNCA triplication). All male (stated in Discussion).
- 60,644 nuclei (10x 3' v3.1): ExN 33.5%, oligodendrocytes 39.8%, InN 11.0%, astrocytes 7.3%, microglia 3.9%, OPC 3.6%, endothelial 0.5%, pericytes 0.3%.
- Bulk validation cohorts: qPCR 11 control / 11 LBD (table S7); Western blot 16 / 16 (table S8).
- LBD clinical subtype (PD / PDD / DLB) of the snRNA cases not given in the main text (table S4).

## Donor metadata available
- Tables S4, S7, S8: Braak NFT stage, Thal amyloid phase, Lewy body counts in cingulate, inferior parietal, middle frontal, parahippocampal, superior temporal gyri and amygdala, Lewy pathology in brainstem/olfactory regions; age, RIN, sequencing saturation (used as DE covariates). PMI and disease duration not mentioned in text.

## Ambiguities / caveats
- DE uses MAST with sample ID as random effect (better than plain cell-level tests) but a very lenient |log2FC|>=0.1; n=5 vs 3.
- Comparison is "LBD vs control" (mixed LBD, includes SNCA multiplication carriers); recorded as such.
- Pathways from IPA (proprietary); directions not given.
- Organoid results (notes only, not claims): SNCA-triplication iPSC cortical organoids show raised total/phospho/aggregated alpha-syn; scRNA-seq of organoids shows synaptic and mitochondrial dysregulation in SNCA-high excitatory neurons (EX1), reduced MEA activity and Seahorse respiration; shared ExN DEGs with brain: FADS1, PRKN, ABCB10 (qPCR-validated in isogenic lines); Parkin protein reduced. RT-QuIC screen of 1,280 FDA drugs found entacapone, tolcapone, phenazopyridine, zalcitabine inhibit alpha-syn seeding and improve mitochondrial function in organoids/neurons.
