## Cohort as reported
- Region: prefrontal cortex, middle frontal gyrus, grey + white matter (Parkinson's UK Brain Bank, Imperial).
- 4 NCI controls (2M/2F), 4 AD (1M/3F), 6 DLB (3M/3F), 6 PDD (3M/3F); age 73-92; no significant age difference.
- 87,470 nuclei after QC (UMAP legend says 90,733); 10x. Composition: ExN 20%, InN 22%, oligodendrocytes 37%, OPC 4%, astrocytes 10%, microglia 4%, endothelial 1%, ependymal 1%.
- No PD without dementia; all LB claims are PDD or DLB vs control.

## Ambiguities / caveats
- DE: union of DESeq2 pseudobulk (sex covariate, nominal P<0.05, no FDR) and MAST on cells (sex + sample covariates, BH<0.01, |log2FC|>1). Figure 2 legend states log2FC>1, FDR<0.01.
- Composition differences are shown as box plots with no named statistical test.
- "Distinct microglia" co-express microglial and oligodendrocyte markers (PLP1, MBP, ST18): could be doublets/ambient myelin RNA; tissue includes white matter.
- Non-sc evidence (notes only): bulk RT-qPCR MSR1 up and MOBP/MAG/MBP down in AD/DLB/PDD PFC (n=20); LRRK2-G2019S mice MSR1 up in primary microglia with MBP/MOBP/PLP1; HMC3 MSR1 overexpression trend to more myelin uptake; myelin exposure raises MSR1 protein; IHC MSR1+ microglia co-localise with MBP in PDD.
- External replication dataset for distinct microglia (ref 59) not identified in the text read.
