# fullard2025 — Population-scale cross-disorder atlas of the human prefrontal cortex at single-cell resolution (PsychAD data descriptor)

- DOI 10.1038/s41597-025-04687-5 · PMID 40480991 · PMCID PMC12144096 · Sci Data 2025
- **Source used:** full text (Europe PMC XML / PMC).

## Cohort as reported
1,494 donors, DLPFC, 6.32 million nuclei in 561 pools, 8 classes and 27 subclasses. Cases include AD n = 519, SCZ 177, **DLBD n = 112**, vascular dementia 85, BD 72, **PD n = 48**, plus many rare disorders. Banks: MSSM, HBCC, RADC. Genotypes: SNP arrays and WGS merged for 1,381 samples.

## Donor metadata available
Harmonised diagnoses (33 diagnoses), CERAD, Braak (NFT), CDR, neuropsychiatric symptoms, age, sex, PMI, ancestry, genotypes. Controls are much younger than neurodegenerative cases; the authors recommend a minimum age of 60 for NDD contrasts.

## Claims
**None.** This is a data descriptor (cohort, QC, genotyping, usage notes) with no biological conclusions about PD or Lewy body disease. `fullard2025.yaml` is an empty list. The PD/DLBD biology from this cohort is in lee2026.
