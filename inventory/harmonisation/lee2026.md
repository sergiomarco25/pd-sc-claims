## Source

## Donor selection rule
Kept:
- Every donor whose CELLxGENE `disease` contains **'Parkinson disease'** (Parkinson_disease = Yes) or **'Lewy body dementia'** (DLBD_status = Yes). They are kept whatever else they carry, e.g. AD, vascular dementia, schizophrenia, PSP or MDD.
- Controls: `disease` = 'normal'. All 261 have every *_status flag = No, so they are neurotypical.

Dropped: everything else, including AD-only, dementia NOS, schizophrenia, vascular, tauopathy, FTD, ALS, head injury and neoplasm. This removes 641 donors.

| kept group | donors |
|---|---|
| PD / PDD | 33 |
| DLB | 107 |
| control | 261 |
| **total** | **401** |

Disease mapping (`disease_raw` keeps the full multi-label string):

| source label | `disease` | donors |
|---|---|---|
| contains 'Lewy body dementia' (PsychAD DLBD) | DLB | 107 |
| 'Parkinson disease' and 'dementia' | PDD | 26 |
| 'Parkinson disease' without 'dementia' | PD | 7 |
| 'normal' | control | 261 |

- In this release the PD and DLBD donor sets are disjoint.
- `comorbidity` lists the other terms, e.g. 'Alzheimer disease', 'schizophrenia' or 'none'. Of the DLB donors, 72 also carry AD.
- PsychAD's own PD contrast allows only AD as a secondary diagnosis. To reproduce it, filter on `comorbidity` ∈ {none, Alzheimer disease}.
- The status flags are kept as `ad_status`, `dlbd_status` and `parkinson_disease_flag`.

## Other obs fields
- `donor` = `sample` = donor_id. Pool information is not in the release.
- `region` = DLPFC.
- `age` comes from `development_stage`:
  - '80 year-old and over stage' (259 donors) is set to 90 and flagged `age_censored`. Ages 81–89 appear explicitly elsewhere, so these donors are most likely ≥ 90.
- `pmi` and `braak_lb` are not in the release (NaN).
- Also kept: `subclass_author`, `genetic_ancestry`, `brain_bank` = MSSM.
