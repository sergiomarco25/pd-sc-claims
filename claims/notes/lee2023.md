# lee2023 — Lee et al. 2023, Science Advances

Single-nucleus transcriptome and epigenome uncover cell type-specific gene dysregulation in Parkinson's disease.
DOI 10.1126/sciadv.abo2467 · PMID 37058563 · PMCID PMC10104466

## Source used
- Europe PMC full-text XML (`/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC10104466/fullTextXML`), converted to text. Abstract, all Results subsections, Discussion, Methods and Fig. 1–6 legends were read.
- Supplementary tables S1–S10 were not opened.

## Cohort as reported
- Region: substantia nigra. In-house tissue from UCSD ADRC. Late-stage PD vs controls; the age difference is not significant (Welch P = 0.803).
- snRNA-seq (10x v3): 19 SN specimens = 6 late-stage PD + 6 control (in-house) + 7 control re-processed from GSE140231 (Agarwal et al. 2020). This gives 6 PD vs 13 controls. 57,270 nuclei after QC (34,638 control, 22,632 PD).
- snATAC-seq (10x ATAC v1): 13 in-house specimens (8 PD + 5 control) + 2 external SN (GSE147672, Corces 2020). 55,937 nuclei (26,074 control, 29,863 PD).
- Bulk data (not single-cell): H3K27ac ChIP-seq on 9 PD + 9 control, in situ Hi-C on 5 PD + 6 control, bulk RNA-seq on 8 PD + 8 control.
- Some libraries pooled 2–3 donors with the same diagnosis and were demultiplexed with souporcell.
- GEO: GSE148434.

## Donor metadata
Table S1 holds sex, age at death, PMI and Braak stage (Braak is scored per Methods). Fig. 2A also shows DRS and MMSE. Disease duration is not mentioned.

## Statistics / pseudoreplication
- DE uses donor pseudobulk per cell type, ComBat-corrected for data source, then edgeR LRT (BH < 0.05, |log2FC| > 0.5, pct > 0.1). Donors are the unit. All external snRNA donors are controls, so data source is confounded with diagnosis.
- Composition uses a one-sided Welch t-test on donor proportions.
- Dysregulated cREs come from BULK H3K27ac ChIP-seq after cellularity correction (edgeR QL). snATAC only assigns the cREs to cell types. The headline "cell type-specific dysregulation in cREs" therefore rests mainly on bulk data.
- The LDSC tests are run on cell-type cRE sets.

## Ambiguities
- The direction of the PD risk-gene DEGs (C08–C10) is given only in the Fig. 1E heatmap and table S3.
- C11, C12, C16, C18 and C19 depend on bulk-defined dysregulated cREs. Re-testing them in snATAC would mean defining differential accessibility per cell type and is not identical to the original analysis.
- C17, C20 and C22 are resource, sequence or synthesis claims and are marked not_testable.
- Excluded as bulk-only: the bulk co-expression modules C1–C9 (Fig. 6), allelic imbalance in ChIP reads (Fig. 4E), and the CRISPR validation in SH-SY5Y (Fig. 3B).
