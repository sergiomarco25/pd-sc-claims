# lee2026 — Single-cell atlas of transcriptomic vulnerability across brain disorders (PsychAD, Nature)

- DOI 10.1038/s41586-025-09573-z · PMID 42778699 · PMCID PMC13600946
- **Source used:** full text (Europe PMC XML); main text, figure legends and the relevant Methods sections. Only PD / DLBD (Lewy body) claims were extracted.

## Cohort as reported
- 1,494 donors, DLPFC only, 6,320,459 nuclei; sources MSSM 1,042, HBCC 300, RADC 152; ages 0–108.
- Cross-disorder tier: 1,160 donors aged ≥17 and 319 neurotypical controls. Contrasts include PD (PD or PD_uncertain_plus_encephalitic; only AD allowed as secondary diagnosis) and DLBD (only AD allowed as secondary). Per-contrast n are in Fig. 1c / Supplementary Table 1. The companion descriptor (fullard2025) gives the cohort totals PD n = 48 and DLBD n = 112.
- Taxonomy: 8 classes, 27 subclasses, 65 subtypes.

## Methods relevant to re-testing
- Composition: crumblr (CLR + precision weights), `~ scale(Age) + Sex + phenotype`.
- DE: dreamlet pseudobulk per tissue source, fixed-effect meta-analysis across sources, then multivariate Bayesian (mashr-type) decomposition into shared and disease-specific effects.

## Ambiguities / caveats
- Most PD-relevant statements are made for "NDDs" as a class (AD, DLBD, Vas, Tau, PD, FTD). PD-specific values must be read from Fig. 4b/4c and Supplementary Table 3, which were not inspected.
- The headline "concordance among AD, DLBD, Vas and PD" is a cross-disease similarity. It is testable only in datasets that also contain AD/Vas cases, or by comparing with published PsychAD effect sizes.
- The genetic claims (LDSC rg; MAPT H1H1 OR = 4.125) are not single-cell. MAPT haplotype can be proxied by ARL17B expression.
- scDRS: the authors summarise neurological GWAS (PD GWAS included in Fig. 2f) as glia/immune-enriched without naming PD, so this was not extracted.
- The AD trajectory, NPS and AD pathology results were not extracted (not PD).
