# xu2023 — Xu et al. 2023, Nature Aging

- DOI 10.1038/s43587-023-00363-8; PMID 36993867; PMCID PMC10046522
- Source used: PMC full text (PubMed MCP tool; italicised gene names were stripped there, so gene names taken from NCBI efetch PMC XML of the same article). Abstract, all Results subsections, Fig. 1-8 and Extended Data Fig. 1-10 legends read. Supplementary tables not opened.

## Cohort as reported
- Region: precommissural putamen (Knight ADRC / WashU MDC brain bank).
- 4 PD, 4 AD, 4 cognitively normal controls (2M/2F each). Ages: PD 69-77 (mean 73), AD 71-82 (mean 75), control 73-85 (mean 79).
- 30,908 nuclei after QC (38,929 before); 10x 3' v3; mean 2,576 nuclei/donor.
- Astrocytes: control 1,203 / AD 1,642 / PD 1,433; immune: 558 / 827 / 619; neurons: 2,259 / 3,343 / 3,286.

## Donor metadata available
- Supplementary Table 1: age, sex, PMI, CDR, Braak Aβ (A/B/C), Braak NFT, Lewy body stage.
- PD: Aβ C (2), B (1), 0 (1); NFT I (2), II (1), III (1); Lewy body stage 6 (all 4). PD cases had CDR >= 1 (dementia criteria applied to PD), i.e. PD cases may be PDD clinically.
- Disease duration not mentioned in the main text.

## Ambiguities / caveats
- All DE is Seurat FindMarkers Wilcoxon on cells (FDR<0.05, |lnFC|>0.25, natural log), 4 vs 4 donors: heavy pseudoreplication.
- "Dementia level was assessed by CDR ... Individuals with a CDR >= 1 were taken": suggests PD donors were demented (PDD-like); comparison recorded as PD vs control as the authors label it.
- Abstract says microglial changes are "largely unique to each disorder" while Results report AD and PD global microglial changes were "highly correlated sharing many downregulated DEGs" (claim C10 flagged).
- Cortical comparisons (ec, pfc) use external AD datasets (Grubman, Lau, Mathys) and Feleke ACC (LBD); regional-divergence claims are mainly AD-based and recorded only where PD-relevant (C09).
- Specific MT family genes in C03 are not enumerated in text.
- TREM2 Micr-1 enrichment in disease (C12) has no formal statistic.
