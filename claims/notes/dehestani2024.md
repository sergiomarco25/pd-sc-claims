# dehestani2024 — Dehestani et al. 2024, Molecular Brain (short report)

- DOI 10.1186/s13041-024-01128-z; PMID 39138468; PMCID PMC11323592
- Source used: Europe PMC full-text XML (open access). Whole text (short report: abstract, combined Results/Discussion, Methods, Fig. 1-3 legends, Table 1) read. Supplementary tables S1-S12 not opened.

## Cohort as reported
- Regions: prefrontal cortex and anterior cingulate cortex from the same donors (pooled in DE).
- 2 LRRK2 p.G2019S PD, 2 GBA1 PD (p.L444P, p.E326K), 2 healthy controls. All male, age 65-80. PD donors Braak Lewy body stage 5-6.
- Netherlands Brain Bank (one LRRK2 from UCL Queen Square).
- 88,876 nuclei after QC (105,781 input); 10x 3' v3.1; ~50% oligodendrocytes, 4.3% OPC, 0.5% vascular.
- Clinical PRS cohort: Tuebingen, 681 PD / 549 controls (N 379-514 with outcome scores); replicate cohort n=358.

## Donor metadata available
- Table S1: clinical characteristics (mutation, age, sex, Braak LB stage; PMI and disease duration presumably there but not stated in text).

## Ambiguities / caveats
- Not sporadic PD: all PD donors carry LRRK2 or GBA1 variants. Comparisons recorded as "GBA1-PD vs control" / "LRRK2-PD vs control"; most replication datasets lack genotype, so these claims are mostly only testable as "PD vs control" approximations.
- n=2 per group; DE is Wilcoxon on cells pooling PFC and ACC nuclei.
- Text names pathways but no individual genes; gene lists only in Tables S5/S7.
- PRS clinical claims use P<0.1 as "significant" and are not testable with snRNA data (need genotype + clinical cohort).
- OPC_ADM increase is cited from Kamath et al., not tested in this paper.
