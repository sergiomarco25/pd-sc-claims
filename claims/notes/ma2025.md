# ma2025 — Ma et al. 2025, Nature Communications

Single-nucleus, spatial and TCR profiling of T cells and glia in the PD substantia nigra and cingulate cortex.
DOI 10.1038/s41467-025-62478-3 · PMID 40759663 · PMCID PMC12322057

## Source used
- Europe PMC full-text XML (`/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC12322057/fullTextXML`), converted to text. The abstract, all Results subsections, Discussion, the relevant Methods (DE, DCATS, LMM, imaging statistics) and the Fig. 1–8 legends were read.
- Supplementary Data 1–11 and the supplementary figures were not opened.

## Cohort as reported
- New York Brain Bank (Columbia). All samples have RIN > 7. 44 donors in total. SN and cingulate samples come from different donors, with no pairing.
- snRNA-seq, SN: 13 PD + 15 controls, 207,859 nuclei (96,244 from PD). 831 T cell nuclei, 535 of them from PD.
- snRNA-seq, cingulate: 10 PD (PDD/DLBD) + 8 controls, 57,425 nuclei (32,442 from PD). No T cells were recovered.
- Astrocytes: SN 10,118 PD / 14,714 control nuclei; cingulate 3,772 PD / 2,904 control.
- SN BAMs: 207 PD / 332 control nuclei.
- TCR-seq, read-based alpha chain: cingulate 11 PD / 6 control; SN 13 PD / 15 control (the Fig. 1c legend says SN 13 / 14).
- TCR-seq, Repseq+ UMI alpha and beta: SN 12 PD / 13 control.
- Visium: 5 PD + 5 control SN sections. These are a subset of the snRNA donors.
- T cell factor donor contributions: 9 PD / 12 control (only donors that have T cells).
- IHC/IF validation: n = 4–8 per group, depending on the stain.
- "Controls" are donors without significant neuropathology, though some have common age-related changes that are not synucleinopathy. Cortical cases are PDD/DLBD, called "PD" in the paper.

## Donor metadata
Supplementary Data 1 holds the demographics. Age, sex and AD-type neuropathologic change / Braak stage are used as covariates. PMI and disease duration are not mentioned in the text.

## Statistics / pseudoreplication
- DE uses limma on nuclei within each lineage, "controlling for donor, binned age and sex". The unit is cells. Including donor as a fixed effect alongside a donor-level condition is not identifiable, so the exact handling is unclear. Only genes with P < 0.05 were taken downstream. Separately, a T cell pseudobulk Wilcoxon test was run (Fig. S3C).
- Composition uses DCATS (beta-binomial GLM on donor × subtype counts) and is donor-level.
- T cell Factor 5 (TRM) is significant at the cell level (Wilcoxon) but not at the donor level (Fig. 3f). Factor 1 is significant at the donor level.
- Visium signature scores use an LMM with a sample random intercept. Visium DE uses limma on spots.
- SCC uses spot-level permutation P values, aggregated per condition.

## Ambiguities
- "CSTD" in the text is presumably CTSD (C11).
- The T cell–activated microglia proximity (C32) is described as validating an SCC increase, but the Fig. 7c list in the text does not name that pair.
- GFAP mRNA is up in SN astrocytes (C18), while IF shows fewer GFAP-high astrocytes in the PD SN.
- MT genes go down in SN astrocytes here but up in Smajić et al. (C20).
- TCR claims (C02, C03) need TCR data. They are marked not_testable for sc/sn RNA datasets.
- CD44 knockdown in mouse astrocytes (Fig. 8) is in-vitro only. It is noted under C25 and not counted as a claim.
