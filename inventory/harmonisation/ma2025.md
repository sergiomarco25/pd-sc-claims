## Source files
- `raw/ma2025/GSE253462_RAW.tar` was extracted to `raw/ma2025/extracted/`. It holds 41 files named `GSM*_<seqid>_{nigra,cingulate}.txt.gz`: 24 SN files (OJ*) and 17 cingulate files (T*).
  - Each file is a **genes × cells** space-separated R `write.table`. The header has the barcodes, one field shorter than the data rows; the first column is the gene symbol.
  - Counts are integers. Full scans of 3 files found 0 non-integers, and the loader asserts integer counts for every chunk.
  - These are the authors' QC-filtered nuclei after ambient removal: CellBender for SN and DecontX for cingulate (GEO data_processing). Cell calling and filtering are the authors'.
  - Cingulate tables have 50,360 genes and nigra tables 36,601, from different references. 31,874 genes are shared. The tables are concatenated with an outer join; genes absent from one reference are 0 (55,087 genes in total). Within-region analyses are unaffected. Cross-region comparisons should use shared genes only.
- `GSE253462_family.soft.gz` gives per sample the donor TB number, tissue, sex, exact age ("89+" stored as 89 with `age_censored_89`=True) and condition. The OJ5 title lacks a TB number; Supplementary Data 1 gives T-2465.
- Supplementary Data 1 (`raw/supplements/ma2025/41467_2025_62478_MOESM3_ESM.xlsx`, sheet A) gives the neuropath diagnosis, age bin, Braak NFT, `path_braak_asyn` and CERAD per donor × sequencing ID.
- No per-nucleus author labels are public; MOESM3 sheet C has only per-sample counts per type. So `AUTHOR_TO_CLASS = {}`, and cells are labelled by `harmonise.annotate_by_markers` (Leiden res 1.0).

## Cohort vs paper (claims/notes/ma2025.md)
| | paper | harmonised |
|---|---|---|
| nuclei | SN 207,859 + cingulate 57,425 = 265,284 | 265,284 (exact) |
| SN donors | 13 PD + 15 control | 11 PD (10 PDD + 1 PD) + 13 control = 24 |
| cingulate donors | 10 PD (PDD/DLBD) + 8 control | 9 PDD + 8 control = 17 |
- The paper's donor counts appear to include donors with TCR-seq only. Supplementary Data 1 marks snRNAseq = N for SN OJ16, OJ19 (PD), OJ8 and OJ17 (control), and for cingulate OF018 (T-4108). So all snRNA libraries are present.
- **T-2465** contributes both an SN library (OJ5) and a cingulate library (OF012). This contradicts the paper's statement that SN and cingulate donors do not overlap. The result is 40 unique donors and 41 donor × region units.
- disease:
  - Condition "PD" with neuropath diagnosis "Parkinson's disease with dementia" maps to **PDD**. This applies to 19 donors, including all cingulate PD cases, consistent with the paper's "PDD/DLBD".
  - Only T-4815 (SN, "Parkinson's disease") stays PD. Pooling PD and PDD as the paper's "PD" group is up to downstream code.
  - Condition "Control" maps to control. Some controls have intermediate AD neuropathological change, PART, or "Other neurological disorder" (T-3799); this is kept in `disease_raw` as "<condition>; <diagnosis>".
- `braak_lb` is NaN. The supplement column `path_braak_asyn` gives 4–6 for several controls (e.g. T-5133 = 6, T-1619 = 6) and 0 for several PDD cases. These values are implausible as Lewy body Braak stages, so they are kept only as `path_braak_asyn_suppl`. `braak_nft` and `cerad` are kept as extra columns.
- pmi is not available (NaN).

## Labels (marker clustering)
- Clusters separate completely by region, because of the different ambient-removal method and depth. Median UMI per nucleus is about 700–2,000 in SN versus 5,000–30,000 in cingulate.
- Assigned classes: Oligodendrocytes 122,709 · Neurons (other) 57,889 · Astrocytes 31,588 · OPCs 16,623 · Immune (other) 13,178 · Microglia 11,709 · Endothelial 5,848 · DA neurons 5,740.
- Label check, cell-weighted 0.895. Clusters with agree < 0.5 are leiden_31 (0.28, "Immune (other)") and leiden_34 (0.32, Microglia).
- Inspection (mean marker scores and log-normalised marker expression per cluster) shows **systematic mislabelling of neurons and myeloid cells**:
  - SN leiden_10 (12,290 nuclei; TH 1.65, SLC6A3 2.22) is clearly **DA neurons** but is labelled "Neurons (other)". Its pan-neuronal score is 2.30 against a DA score of 1.78. Only leiden_12 (5,740) was labelled DA, so DA neurons are undercounted about 3×.
  - SN leiden_11 and 13 (GAD1/GAD2+) and cingulate leiden_5 and 6 (GAD1/GAD2+) are inhibitory neurons, labelled "Neurons (other)".
  - Cingulate leiden_3 and 4 (SLC17A7/SATB2+) are excitatory neurons, labelled "Neurons (other)".
  - SN leiden_31 (13,178; PTPRC 1.7, CD163 0.8, P2RY12 0.2) is microglia/myeloid, labelled "Immune (other)". Its Microglia and Immune scores are 0.44 and 0.48.
  - No T-cell cluster appears; the paper reports only 831 T cells in SN.
- Cause: `harmonise.annotate_by_markers` takes the argmax over all classes. The pan-neuronal "Neurons (other)" set (SNAP25/SYT1/RBFOX3/STMN2) always beats the subtype sets in neurons. Reported to the coordinator as a harmonise.py issue (not edited). Until it is fixed, ma2025 neuron-subtype and DA-neuron results should not be used. Microglia counts also exclude leiden_31.
