## Source and conversion
- GEO GSE303823 provides `GSE303823_rna_annotated.rds`, a Seurat v4 object with assays RNA (v4 `Assay`) and SCT, and reductions pca and umap.
- It was converted in env `mast_rev`, without SeuratObject, by reading slots through `attr()`.
- The RNA `counts` were exported to `raw/chia2025/converted/` as `counts.mtx.gz`, `genes.tsv`, `barcodes.tsv` and `meta.tsv`.
  - Counts are integer UMI (0 non-integers): 33,538 genes × 87,470 nuclei.
  - These are the authors' QC'd singlets. Both DoubletFinder and scDblFinder flag every nucleus as singlet.
- meta.data has `sample`, `group` (CTRL/ADD/DLB/PDD), `sex` and `cell.type`.

## Cohort vs paper
| | paper | harmonised |
|---|---|---|
| nuclei | 87,470 (UMAP legend: 90,733) | 87,470 |
| donors | 4 NCI (2M/2F), 4 AD (1M/3F), 6 DLB (3M/3F), 6 PDD (3M/3F) | same counts and sex split |
| region | PFC, middle frontal gyrus (grey + white matter) | `PFC` |
- One library per donor, so donor equals sample, using the GEO IDs CTRL_4–7, ADD_4–7, DLB_2/4/5/6/7/8 and PDD_1/2/4/5/7/9. The meta uses "DLB_4" and "DLB_8" where GEO titles have "DLB4" and "DLB8".
- disease: CTRL → control, ADD → AD, DLB → DLB, PDD → PDD (the source states dementia). `disease_raw` holds the meta group.
- Supplementary Table S1/S3 use other IDs (NCI_1–4, AD_1–4, DLB_1–5,7, PDD_1–6). The two are matched in order within each group; the match is in `suppl_id`.
  - **Verified:** the per-cell-type counts are identical, e.g. ADD_4 = AD_1 with 1,956 astrocytes and 3,766 ExN, and CTRL_4 = NCI_1 with 1,299 astrocytes. Only the "All cell types" totals in S1 differ slightly.
- `braak_nft` comes from S1 "B&B stage", which is Braak & Braak NFT stage and was given for 11 donors. `braak_lb` is NaN: S3 describes α-syn pathology in prose without a stage.
- age and pmi are NaN; only group means are reported (Table S2: NCI 86.3, AD 82.5, DLB 77.5, PDD 79.5).

## Labels
`author_cell_type` = `cell.type`. All 8 types map one-to-one: Oligodendrocyte, Inhibitory/Excitatory neuron, Astrocyte, OPC, Microglia, Ependymal cell → Ependymal, Endothelial cell → Endothelial. The authors' Seurat cluster is kept in `seurat_cluster_author`.

Cells per class: Oligodendrocytes 32,021 · Inhibitory 19,215 · Excitatory 17,671 · Astrocytes 8,905 · OPCs 3,852 · Microglia 3,620 · Ependymal 1,139 · Endothelial 1,047.

Label check (cell-weighted 0.569):
- Glia agree: astrocytes 0.96, ependymal 0.99, OPC 0.96, ODC 0.95, microglia 0.56, endothelial 0.52.
- Excitatory (0.12) and inhibitory (0.06) neurons have marker majority "Neurons (other)". This is the same `label_check` effect as in dehestani2024: pan-neuronal markers outscore the subtype sets. It is not a labelling error.

## Caveats for downstream tests
- Neuronal composition is very uneven across DLB and PDD donors. Several have **almost no excitatory neurons**: DLB_5 0, DLB_7 0, DLB_8 0, PDD_2 0, PDD_7 5, DLB_2 3. Other donors have thousands. This points to sampling differences (grey vs white matter) or to library quality.
- Several PDD and DLB libraries are very small (DLB_4 281, PDD_1 390, PDD_2 303, PDD_4 327 nuclei).
- Disease-vs-control composition and neuronal claims are therefore confounded by sampling.
