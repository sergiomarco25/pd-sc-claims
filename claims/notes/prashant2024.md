# prashant2024 — AMP-PD multiregion snRNA-seq + WGS data descriptor

- DOI 10.1038/s41597-024-04117-y · PMID 39580497 · PMCID PMC11585549 · Sci Data 2024
- **Source used:** full text (Europe PMC XML / PMC).

## Cohort as reported
- 100 donors: **75 PD** spanning Braak PD stages and **25 controls**; 62 M / 38 F; mostly European ancestry. Banks: Udall (21, all PD), Harvard (25), UMiami NBB (17), UMiami BEB (13), Mount Sinai (24).
- 5 regions: DMNX, GPi, PMC (BA4), DLPFC (BA9), PVC (BA17). snRNA-seq (10x 3' v3.1, hashtag multiplexed, 6 donors per pool) for 97 donors; 71 donors have all 5 regions; 2,096,155 nuclei after QC; 9 major classes. WGS for all 100.
- Per-region PD/control counts are not given in the text (Fig. 2a / 3e).

## Donor metadata available
Braak PD (LB) stage, Braak AD (83% of donors), Hoehn & Yahr, CERAD, PD medical/family history, smoking/alcohol, and MDS-UPDRS, MMSE, Schwab & England and Epworth mainly for Udall donors. Also age, sex, PMI, RIN, ancestry and WGS genotypes. The authors recommend adjusting for sex, age, bank, RIN and PMI.

## Claims
This is a data descriptor with no PD-vs-control biological analysis. One weak claim was extracted: early-affected regions yield fewer nuclei (Fig. 3f; no test reported). Two items were not extracted: the Braak PD stage vs clinical phenotype correlation (Fig. 2h; clinical, not single-cell), and the 9 cell classes (atlas statistic).
