# wang2024: Wang et al. 2024, Sci Adv, doi:10.1126/sciadv.adi8287, PMID 38198537

**Source used:** Europe PMC full-text XML for PMC10780895. Read the abstract, all Results subsections, the figure legends, Table 1 and the Methods for composition, DE and enrichment.

**Cohort as reported**
- SN snRNA-seq (10x 3'): 23 idiopathic PD and 9 controls, 315,867 high-quality nuclei (248,245 PD and 67,622 control).
- IHC/IF cohorts are separate: 5 PD vs 5 controls for RIT2/TH counts (table S1).
- Midbrain organoid scRNA-seq and mouse RNAscope were used as validation and are not recorded as claims.

**Donor metadata (Table 1)**
- Age: controls 83.8 ± 8.3; PD 78.8 ± 7.6.
- Male: controls 4/9 (44%); PD 17/23 (73.9%).
- PMI: controls 17.3 ± 6.3 h; PD 19.1 ± 8.5 h.
- PD Braak stages: II 4, III 3, IV 5, VI 8, NA 3.
- Disease duration is not reported in the main text.

**Ambiguities and caveats**
- Composition used a one-tailed Wilcoxon on per-donor odds, so the unit is donors. One control contributes more than 30% of control c9 cells.
- DE used MAST on cells with PMI, age and sex covariates and no donor random effect, which is pseudoreplication. Thresholds: Bonferroni and FC ≥ 20%.
- c9 (RIT2-enriched, mostly TH−) shares the CADPS2 marker with the smajic2022 CADPS2high cluster, but the reported directions are opposite.
- The text says DNAJC6 is down "in c7_3 or c9" and also up in c9.
- The TYROBP network comes from IPA, which is proprietary.
- The Braak-stage analysis is cell-level, and the early stage has only 7 PD donors.
- In CellChat, PD and control cells were pooled, with no replication statistic.
